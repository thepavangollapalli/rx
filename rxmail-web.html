Important information

This is a fictional demonstration website for the invented brand "RxMail." It is not a real pharmacy or telehealth provider, sells nothing, and provides no medical care. The content below imitates the structure of a real prescription-service safety section for realism only.

Who telehealth may not be right for

Online care isn't appropriate for medical emergencies. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number. Some conditions and medications require in-person evaluation or lab work before they can be prescribed.

About prescriptions

In a real service, a prescription is issued only if a licensed clinician determines it's appropriate after reviewing your intake. Not everyone who requests a treatment will be prescribed one. Availability of specific medications varies by state, and some states restrict or prohibit certain telehealth prescribing.

Common considerations

  • Tell your clinician about all medications, supplements, and allergies.
  • Some treatments interact with other drugs or existing conditions.
  • Report side effects to your clinician; seek urgent care for severe reactions.
